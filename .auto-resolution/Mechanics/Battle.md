# Battle Mechanics

## Determining battle order

### Initiative vs speed

The difference between speed and initiative is in *who* is battling. If there is a Pokemon entering battle, this roll is referred to as a "speed" roll. Any Pokemon get their own entry in the speed table.
If only PCs are battling then it is considered a roll for initiative.
In either case, it is handled in the same way— roll a D20, and add your initiative bonus. Highest goes first.
